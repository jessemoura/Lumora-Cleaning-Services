Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Lumora Cleaning Services ("we", "us", "our"), we are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or use our services in Bristol, UK, in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following data about you:

  • Contact Information: Name, phone number (e.g., via WhatsApp), and property address when you request a quote or book a cleaning service.
  • Communication Data: Records of correspondence when you contact us via WhatsApp, phone, or email.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and basic analytics data collected through essential cookies when you visit our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data to:

  • Provide and manage our cleaning services.
  • Communicate with you regarding quotes, scheduling, and customer support.
  • Improve our website and services based on user interaction.

We operate entirely via WhatsApp for bookings and communication to ensure a fast, encrypted, and direct experience for our clients.

3. Data Sharing and Security

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only when necessary to fulfill our service (e.g., providing your address to our cleaning staff) or if required by law.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure your data is secure against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

4. Cookies

Our website uses basic cookies to enhance your browsing experience. You can choose to accept or decline cookies through the banner on our homepage. Declining cookies will not prevent you from using our website.

5. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request the deletion of your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
